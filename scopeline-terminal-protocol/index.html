the pipeline

STAGE 1

Recon

Maps the attack surface. Read-only, no exploitation.

STAGE 2

Scan

Flags candidates. Nothing is called vulnerable yet.

STAGE 3

Validate

Reproduces each candidate. Unconfirmed items stay marked, not deleted.

STAGE 4

Report

Plain-English summary, evidence, fixes — validated findings only.
