How an engagement runs

1

Recon

Maps the attack surface. Read-only, no exploitation.

2

Scan

Flags candidates. Nothing is called vulnerable yet.

3

Validate

Reproduces each candidate by hand. Unconfirmed stays marked, not deleted.

4

Report

Plain-English summary, evidence, fixes. Validated findings only.

Not a scanner with a logo on it.

A

Every candidate finding is reproduced before it's reported — never assumed from a scanner's output.

B

Items tested but clean are disclosed, so you know exactly what was checked — not just what was found.

C

No testing begins without signed written authorization. No exceptions, ever.
